Privacy policy

homelab-backup is a private tool used only by its owner's household. This policy explains how it handles data.

What it accesses

Only files that homelab-backup itself creates in the owner's Google Drive (Google permission drive.file). It cannot read, list or change any other files, email, contacts or account information.

What it stores

Encrypted backup archives of the owner's own home server. Files are encrypted on the owner's server before upload; Google and anyone else see only scrambled data.

Sharing

No data is collected from other people, and nothing is shared, sold, or used for advertising or analytics. There are no cookies or tracking on this website.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the backup folder in Google Drive removes all stored data.
